Tallybook notice, version 4

Before you install - please read this

Tallybook is a private tool for our group of friends. It is not made, approved or supported by Blizzard. This notice says what it does today. If that ever changes, the tray app shows you the new version and asks again before it carries on.

WHAT THE TRAY APP DOES
It reads one file that the Tallybook addon saves - your auction house scans, and what the addon has learned about recipes and vendor prices - and sends that file to our group's server. That file also says which recipes each of your characters knows, and their skill rank in each profession. Each character is listed under a scrambled id, never under its name.

It writes one data file back into the Tallybook addon's own folder, called Data.lua, so your game has the group's prices without you scanning. Your own shopping list and your own Tallybook settings go into that same file. Your Data.lua is made for you and goes only to your PCs.

When you open your mailbox, the addon notes your own auction sales and returns - the item, the count, the price, the deposit and the auction house's cut - and never who bought it or anyone's name. You see your sales in detail; the group sees only totals per item. This is on unless you switch "Record my sales" off on the Your PCs page of the website. Switch it off and the server keeps none from then on; the addon stops reading them at your next login or Sync.

It also installs the Tallybook addon for you, and replaces it when a newer version comes out, so you never have to copy folders about. It writes nothing anywhere else in your game, and it keeps your own prices when it updates. You can switch that off in Settings, and Settings can also put the addon back from the public code on GitHub, without asking our server at all.

That is all. It does not touch the game while it runs, and it never sends your account name, your character names or any password. It reads nothing else on your PC, with one exception that is not about you: on the owner's own PC only, it also reads the game's item cache file - eleven tables about items, such as their names and stats - so the website can describe every item. The server asks for that from the owner's install alone; nobody else's tray app ever reads it.

WHAT THE ADDON DOES BY ITSELF
When you open the auction house, Tallybook runs ONE quick price scan by itself - once per visit, and not at all if someone in the group scanned in the last 30 minutes. It never posts, bids, buys or cancels. To stop it, untick "auto-scan" on the Tallybook strip beside the auction house window; the scan buttons keep working either way.

LATER
Switches for your gold, your purchases, what your characters carry in bags and bank, and your own auctions MAY come later. Each one will be off until you turn it on, and each will be described here, in a new version of this notice, before it ships. This notice does not cover any of them.

THE RISK
Blizzard's terms do not give permission for programs like this. Well-known tools - the TradeSkillMaster desktop app, for one - have worked the same way for years without Blizzard acting against the people who use them, so the realistic chance of trouble is very small. It is not zero. If Blizzard ever did act, it could affect your whole Battle.net account, not just this game.

YOUR CHOICE
You can use the addon on its own with no tray app, and upload your file by hand on the website - or not upload at all. With the tray app you can switch off "Bring data back", pause it or quit it whenever you like, and remove it by deleting it.

It is free, and it stays free. Nobody may charge for it or pass it on. One Battle.net account each, and anyone who buys gold is out of the group.

AT LAUNCH
Tallybook keeps running when World of Warcraft: Forever launches on 4 November; there is nothing to reinstall. Everything gathered during the beta - prices, scans, sales and known recipes - is wiped on 3 November, and the group starts again from nothing at launch.
